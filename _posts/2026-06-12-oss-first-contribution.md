This is a short follow up on previous [post]({% post_url 2026-06-07-starting-on-oss %}). I began my OSS journey in a way I was not expecting, but finally my first [PR](https://github.com/bcgit/bc-csharp/pull/680){:target="blank" rel="noopener"}. was merged.

As mentioned before, the OSS guide (link in the previous post), has offered me different approaches to tackle this endeavor; one tip is about interacting with the community.

I tagged the maintainers in my contributions, seeking feedback, and it worked for Bouncy Castle. Without any friction, my contribution was just accepted. 

This happened while I was writing about my initial experience. I even thought about rewriting the first post, however I'd have masked the real struggles I faced at the time.

Whether you like it or not, software is a social activity, so please communicate! 

Working in a cave is not always healthy (though sometimes necessary). You need to go out there and speak if you want to make it happen!
